Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Reassured is built so that your audits stay yours. This policy explains what the app does with information, in plain language.

The short version

  • We don't collect your data. Reassured has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no advertising.
  • Everything you enter (audits, notes, photos, evidence, clients, and settings) is stored only on your device.
  • Data leaves your device only when you choose to share it, for example by exporting a report or saving a backup file.

Information stored on your device

Audits, control results, notes, findings, tasks, clients, Evidence Locker documents, photos, and your settings are kept in the app's storage on your device. iOS encrypts this storage, and Reassured sets it to be unreadable while your device is locked. Your Reassured PIN is never stored as typed; a salted, one-way hash is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device only.

If your device is backed up to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include the app's data in that device backup, under Apple's terms and your backup settings.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to photograph evidence and scan documents. Photos stay in the app on your device.
  • Photos: only the photos you pick are imported. Reassured doesn't browse your library.
  • Face ID or Touch ID: only if you turn on the app lock or the Evidence Locker lock. Apple handles the check; Reassured never sees biometric data.
  • Notifications: reminders for task due dates and expiring evidence are scheduled on your device.

Apple Intelligence

On supported devices, Reassured can use Apple's on-device model to help draft findings and summaries. This runs on your device, and Reassured does not send that content to us or to any other server.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a small summary (score, audit name, and next tasks) that the app keeps on your device. Client names and task titles are hidden on the Lock Screen while your device is locked.

Sharing and exports

Reports, certificates, audit exports, and backup files are created on your device. They leave it only when you share or save them, using the destination you choose (for example Mail, Files, or AirDrop). Backup files contain your audit data and evidence, so keep them somewhere private.

Contacting us

If you email us for help, we receive what you send, such as your email address and message, and use it only to answer you. If you've set a help contact in Reassured's settings, the Get Help button opens your own Mail app addressed to that contact.

Children

Reassured is a business tool and isn't directed to children. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If Reassured ever adds features that send data off your device, such as accounts or sync, this policy will be updated before that release, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
