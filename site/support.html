Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Reassured has no accounts or servers. To move audits to another device, use Settings, then Backup and Restore.

How do I back up my audits?

Open Settings, then Backup and Restore, then Create Backup. Save the file to Files, iCloud Drive, or your computer. Restoring adds anything that isn't already on the device and never deletes or duplicates audits.

I deleted an audit by mistake.

Deleted audits wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Open the Audits tab, scroll to Recently Deleted at the bottom, and tap Restore.

I forgot my Reassured PIN.

On the PIN screen, tap Forgot PIN, confirm it's you with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode, then choose a new PIN.

Why does my audit say "too early" instead of a score?

An audit shows a score once at least 5 controls are answered Pass, Partial, or Fail, so a couple of early answers don't read as 100%.

Is a Reassured report or certificate a compliance certification?

No. Reports and certificates record the assessment that was done. They aren't a certification or attestation of compliance with any law, regulation, or standard. Formal certifications, like a CMMC Level 2 certification or a SOC 2 report, come from authorized assessors.

Why don't I see the Apple Intelligence writing buttons?

They appear only on devices that support Apple Intelligence, with it turned on in Settings.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Reassured. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add a Reassured widget.
